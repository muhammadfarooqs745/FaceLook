Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

You can visit the "Forgot password" screen or go to Settings > Security & Login > Change Password at any time.

How do 24-hour stories work?

Photos you share to your story appear at the top of your friends' feeds and automatically expire after 24 hours.

How can I report abusive content or spam?

Click the three dots (•••) on any post and choose "Report Post". Our admin moderation team will promptly review it.
